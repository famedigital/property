Why Pemakhang (not bare Pema)

Pema alone collides with hotels, “Pema Realm”, clinics, and common given names. Pemakhang = Pema (lotus) + khang (house / building) — PEMA kept, meaning locked to this property product.

Alternate shorter mark: Pemora. Avoid leading with bare “Pema” as the product name.
